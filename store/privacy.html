Privacy Policy — Nofollow Highlighter

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Nofollow Highlighter ("the extension") is designed to be privacy-preserving by default.

What data we collect

None. The extension does not collect, store remotely, transmit, sell, or share any personal data or browsing data. There are no analytics, no telemetry, no tracking, no cookies, and no remote code.

How the extension works

The extension runs entirely on your device (locally, inside your browser). To highlight links, it reads the content (DOM) of the page you are currently viewing in order to classify each link by its rel attribute (nofollow, sponsored, ugc, dofollow). This processing happens in real time, in the page itself, and the results are never sent anywhere. The extension makes no network requests of its own.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — Used only to save your own settings locally in the browser: whether highlighting is on, the marking style, which link types are shown, the "external links only" option, and the list of sites you chose to exclude. These settings stay on your device and are never transmitted.
  • Host access (http/https pages) — Required so the extension can read the links on the pages you open and color them in place. It is used solely to classify and highlight links; no page content or browsing history is collected, stored remotely, or shared.

Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use or transfer user data for any purpose unrelated to the extension's single purpose (highlighting links for SEO review). We do not use user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Children's privacy

The extension does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a new "Last updated" date.
